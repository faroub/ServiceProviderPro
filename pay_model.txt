Great question — and it's the **hardest** part of running a marketplace in a cash-heavy economy. Here's an honest, practical breakdown.

---

## The core problem

Once a client and provider connect on your app, they can just:
1. Exchange phone numbers via chat
2. Complete the job offline
3. Pay in cash
4. Skip your commission entirely

This is called **disintermediation** (or "leakage") and every marketplace fights it. Airbnb, Uber, Upwork all had to solve it. Your job is to make the app-mediated path **cheaper, safer, and more convenient** than going around it.

---

## Enforcement models — ranked from soft to hard

### 🟢 Soft enforcement (easy, honor-system)

**How it works:** Provider marks booking "completed" → app calculates commission → provider owes you a monthly bill → you send an invoice.

**Pros:** Zero payment integration. Ship this week.
**Cons:** Providers will underreport. Expect **50–70% leakage**.

**When it works:** Only after providers *trust* you enough that they don't want to lose the platform.

---

### 🟡 Medium enforcement (in-app payments, providers pay you)

**How it works:**
1. Client & provider agree on price in the app.
2. When booking is marked `completed`, provider is charged commission (10–15%) via saved payment method.
3. If they don't pay within 7 days → account auto-deactivated.

**Pros:** Automated, transparent.
**Cons:** Providers still take cash from clients, so they must **actively pay you afterward**. Some will let their account die rather than pay.
**Leakage:** ~20–40%.

---

### 🟠 Strong enforcement (client pays through app, escrow-style)

**How it works:**
1. Client pays the **full amount** (say 3000 DZD) into your platform via CIB/BaridiMob/Chargily when booking is confirmed.
2. Money is held in escrow.
3. After job completion, provider gets 2700 DZD released (you keep 300 DZD = 10%).
4. Cash discouraged — provider is told "you'll be paid within 24h of client confirming."

**Pros:** You physically hold the money → commission is impossible to skip. Also builds *client* trust (they can dispute).
**Cons:** Requires a real DZ payment gateway. Slower onboarding. Some clients don't have online payment.
**Leakage:** <10%.

**This is the Uber/Airbnb model** and it's the only one that scales.

---

### 🔴 Hardest enforcement (mandatory in-app payment before contact info reveal)

Same as above, but you **hide the provider's phone number until the client has paid**. All communication happens through in-app chat + optional in-app calling.

**Leakage:** <5%. But UX friction is high for a market that expects direct phone contact.

---

## My recommended combo for Algeria

Given the cash reality + low card penetration, I'd stack these:

### Phase 1 (month 1–3): **Soft + tracking** (validate the model)
- Every completed booking accrues commission in a `provider_debt` field
- Providers see running balance in profile
- Show them the value first (leads, verified badge, reviews) before charging anything

### Phase 2 (month 3–9): **Medium + auto-deactivation**
- Provider owes commission for completed bookings
- Payment options: CIB card via Chargily, BaridiMob (Algérie Poste), or manual bank transfer with proof upload
- 7-day grace period → then auto-deactivate account (same infra as your subscription flow)
- Reactivation = pay outstanding balance

### Phase 3 (month 9+): **Strong for high-value bookings only**
- Bookings > 5000 DZD → client pays in app (escrow)
- Smaller bookings → provider pays commission after
- Gradually shift more bookings into escrow as trust grows

## Anti-leakage tactics (all models)

These reduce leakage regardless of payment model:

1. **Hide phone numbers behind first booking**
   - Provider sees client phone only after client hits "Confirm booking"
   - Client sees provider phone only after provider hits "Accept"
   - Log the reveal; if provider is confirmed but no completion happens repeatedly, flag them

2. **Detect chat leakage patterns**
   - Scan chat messages server-side for phone numbers, WhatsApp/Telegram/Viber names, "let's do it outside", "cash direct"
   - Warn once, then throttle
   - The threat matters more than actual enforcement

3. **Rate providers on completion rate, not just star reviews**
   - Provider who has 20 confirmed bookings but 0 completed = obvious cheater
   - Auto-lower their search ranking

4. **Bonus for using in-app payment**
   - Verified providers who use in-app payments 10× in a month → featured badge, priority placement, lower commission
   - Positive incentives beat threats

5. **Client-side dispute button**
   - "I paid the provider but he asked me to cancel the booking" → refund + provider penalty
   - Gives clients a channel that hurts cheaters

6. **Random completion audits**
   - Send SMS 24h after "completed" booking: "Reply YES if [Provider Name] actually did the job."
   - Cheap way to catch fake completions

## Concrete technical plan for your app

Here's what I'd build, in dependency order:

### Step 1 — Commission tracking (backend)

Add fields to bookings + a new collection:

```python
# On booking creation:
"platform_commission_pct": 10,             # locked in at booking time
"platform_commission_dzd": 300,            # 10% of 3000
"payment_flow": "provider_owes",           # or "escrow" later
"commission_paid": False,
"commission_paid_at": None,
```

New collection `provider_debts`:
```python
{
  "provider_id": "...",
  "total_owed_dzd": 2400,
  "bookings": [<booking_ids>],
  "last_paid_at": None,
  "grace_ends_at": "2026-07-15T...",
}
```

### Step 2 — Enforcement hook

On `PATCH /api/bookings/{id}/status` when status → `completed`:
- Compute commission, add to `provider_debts.total_owed_dzd`
- Reuse your existing `enforce_lifecycle` pattern — if `total_owed_dzd > threshold AND grace_ends_at < now` → mark provider `is_manually_deactivated=true`
- Provider must pay to reactivate

### Step 3 — Payment integration (Chargily is the fastest DZ on-ramp)

**Chargily Pay** (https://chargily.com) supports CIB + Edahabia + BaridiMob. Free to integrate, ~2.5% transaction fee. Similar to Stripe.

Two new endpoints:
- `POST /api/commission/pay` → creates Chargily invoice, returns checkout URL
- `POST /api/webhooks/chargily` → receives payment confirmation, clears debt

### Step 4 — Anti-leakage layer (chat scanner)

Simple regex on outgoing chat messages:

```python
LEAKAGE_PATTERNS = [
    r"\+?213\d{9}",           # DZ phone
    r"0[567]\d{8}",           # DZ mobile
    r"whats?app|viber|telegram|imo",
    r"(en dehors|cash direct|hors app|بغير التطبيق)",
]
```

On match → replace message with `[hidden — please use in-app booking]` and log incident.

### Step 5 — Escrow (later)

Only when you have >100 monthly bookings and Chargily is working smoothly. Requires:
- Payout account for each provider (bank account or Edahabia number)
- Manual or automated payout job (weekly)
- Regulatory: talk to a DZ lawyer, escrow accounts have licensing implications
